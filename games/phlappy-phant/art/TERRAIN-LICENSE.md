# Terrain artwork licence

`art/terrain.png` is cut from **Seasonal Platformer Tiles** by **GrafxKid**
(https://opengameart.org/content/seasonal-platformer-tiles), released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
public domain dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The author asks to be credited as
GrafxKid; this file and the game's About menu do so.

`art/terrain-parallax.png` is a copy of that layout with the backdrop painted as flat bands marking its parallax
layers, and with the tree and shrub touched up so they sit on the 8-pixel grid. The backdrop, river animation, tower,
ground and scenery tiles in `assets/backdrop.png`, `assets/tower.png`, `assets/ground.png` and `assets/scenery.png`,
and the scenery on `poster.png`, are cut from these two sheets by `scripts/generate-phant-sprites.mjs`. They carry no
further restriction beyond CC0.
