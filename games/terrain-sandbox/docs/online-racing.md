# Race another driver

Place road gates or generate a dry circuit, then choose **Online racing** in Explore or the pause menu. **Preview current circuit** shows the start, route, lighting, weather and lap count. A challenge can have 1 to 20 laps. To race an endless local circuit, check **Set laps for a challenge copy** and enter a lap count.

## Race live

Sign in with Matrix. **Open a table** invites anyone in the room. **Challenge** invites a particular driver. You can preview another driver's course before joining. Both drivers download the same landscape, edits, resolved route and gates. The countdown starts after both drivers are on the grid and their browsers have connected.

Drive with the ordinary car controls. Both cars start in the same place and pass through each other. Checkpoints, road recovery and penalties use the same rules on both machines. The result shows both times. **Rematch** invites the same driver to the same course. **Watch** opens a seat as a spectator. A brief network interruption lets both browsers reconnect and fill in missing inputs. Closing the driving page mid-run ends that continuous attempt. A completed drive can be resubmitted after a reload when browser storage is available.

## Leave a ghost for later

Choose **Record a challenge** and finish the course. **Save recorded run** downloads the course and driving inputs together. **Post my ghost** publishes them to the room. Once the upload finishes, the author can close the game.

Select a ghost and choose **Preview ghost** to download it and replay the drive through the car physics. **Race selected ghost** puts both cars on the grid. The clock shows how your checkpoint times compare. A faster finish unlocks **Post my ghost**. Each reply keeps a link to the run it beat. Drivers who beat the same record can both publish. If the homeserver removes the uploaded media, **Restore missing media** accepts an exact saved copy.

**Open saved run** imports a competitive recording for verification. Ordinary sandbox demos, including branched recordings, remain available in the demo player. A verified result establishes that the recorded inputs drove a car through the required gates.

## Connect a room

The default race room is `#terrain-sandbox:matrix.org`. A room administrator can create that public room and add it to the Phonics discovery Space. Hosts can point the terrain sandbox's Matrix configuration at their own room. Local recording and saving work without a Matrix account.
