Aventador engine and exhaust
Super Cars for you, Erwin Lindsen, and ePic eXhaust recorded the three sources.
Their original titles, URLs and preparation credits are listed in manifest.json
alongside the loops. The ready-made RPM and throttle layers come from
https://github.com/justinagaudelli-eng/supercar-engine-sound/tree/b6dc7d5ef3492a0a42cdd52639eb9bfab2c08a4c/public/samples/svj
The sandbox ships those WAVs as supplied and varies pitch and gain during playback.
